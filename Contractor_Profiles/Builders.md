## QUESTION 2: WHAT DO THEY BUY ELSEWHERE
## THAT THEY SHOULD BE BUYING FROM YOU?
HIGH PROBABILITY GAPS — categories builders
commonly source from lumber yards and specialty
suppliers instead of Home Depot:
Framing lumber in volume — builders often
have direct accounts with lumber yards for
framing packages.
Opportunity: Home Depot Pro pricing on
framing lumber is competitive for smaller
builders and infill builders doing
1-5 units at a time. Delivery to jobsite
removes the lumber yard logistics burden.
Engineered lumber — LVL beams and I-joists
often quoted through lumber yard packages.
Opportunity: Home Depot stocks and can
order engineered lumber. For builders
doing smaller projects this is often
faster than a full package quote.
Concrete and masonry — frequently purchased
at masonry supply or concrete suppliers.
Opportunity: Bagged concrete, block,
mortar, and rebar for foundation and
flatwork are available at Pro pricing.
Insulation — often purchased at insulation
distributors.
Opportunity: Batt insulation for framing
stages is a natural add-on to lumber orders.
Interior finish materials — paint, flooring,
trim, and doors are often purchased late
in the project at retail pricing.
Opportunity: Get in front of finish
material selection at framing stage.
Do not wait for them to call you
when they need paint.
Appliances — frequently purchased at
appliance dealers or big box retail
at retail pricing.
Opportunity: Home Depot Pro appliance
pricing on volume orders for builders
doing multiple units.

## QUESTION 3: OUTCOMES THEY CARE ABOUT
MARGIN AND EXIT VALUE
Builders in Northeast Atlanta are building
for sale or rental yield. Every dollar of
material cost saved goes directly to their
exit margin or cash-on-cash return.
Insight angle: Help them understand total
project material cost at framing stage,
not just lumber. Full project visibility
early protects their pro forma.
DRAW SCHEDULE ALIGNMENT
Builders draw from construction loans on
a schedule tied to inspection milestones.
Materials need to be available when the
draw funds, not before and not after.
Insight angle: Understand their draw
schedule and align delivery timing
to it. This is a high-value service
no distributor offers systematically.
TIMELINE — CARRYING COST REDUCTION
Every extra week a builder carries a lot
costs them in interest, taxes, and
opportunity cost. Faster material
availability reduces cycle time.
Insight angle: Same-day availability
and reliable delivery reduces the
schedule risk that drives carrying cost.
SUBCONTRACTOR COORDINATION
Builders coordinate multiple subs —
framers, plumbers, electricians, roofers.
Material availability failures affect
multiple subcontractors simultaneously.
Insight angle: Position yourself as the
single point of contact for all material
categories. One call covers lumber,
roofing, finish, and appliances.
CREDIT TERMS
Builders are capital-intensive.
Construction loan draws fund materials
but timing gaps create cash flow pressure.
Insight angle: Commercial credit bridges
draw timing gaps and reduces the builder's
need to use construction loan funds
for material float.
